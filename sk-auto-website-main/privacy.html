Effective Date: August 15, 2026

S&K Auto respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our website, request service, schedule an appointment, contact us, or consent to receive text messages.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

SMS/Text Messaging

If you voluntarily opt in to receive SMS messages from S&K Auto, we may send you appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, service-related updates, and customer-care messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

SMS consent and phone numbers collected for SMS communication purposes will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing of Information

S&K Auto does not sell or rent your personal information. We may use service providers when necessary to operate our website, appointment system, communications, or other business services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request corrections to personal information you have provided. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to a text message from us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
