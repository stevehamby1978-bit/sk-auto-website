Yes. Garavex is a multi-shop system designed to keep each shop’s customers, vehicles, employees and repair data separated.

Your shop connects its own Stripe account through Garavex. Customers can then pay online through your connected payment setup.

Yes. Garavex supports secure customer-facing authorization links for recommended repair approval.
