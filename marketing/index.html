Questions

Built to make onboarding straightforward.

Does each shop have its own Garavex account?

Yes. Garavex is designed as a multi-shop system with shop-specific data and employee access.

How do card payments work?

The shop connects Stripe through Garavex and completes Stripe's onboarding. Customer card payments are then handled through that shop's connected Stripe setup.

Can customers approve recommended repairs?

Yes. Garavex supports customer-facing authorization links for recommended repair approval.

Is Garavex only for large shops?

No. Garavex is being built around the workflow of independent automotive repair businesses.
